Fixing Spatial Label Noise & <1% Class Imbalance in Histopathology

During chemical preparation, thin gigapixel biopsy tissue warps physically. Standard intensity-based registration scripts failed completely, feeding misaligned patches into our Pix2Pix and DCLGAN networks and causing blurry, hallucinated cell borders.

  • 01. 160–180 Control-Point Registration: Engineered a manual coordinate registration workflow in QuPath and Fiji (ImageJ) to achieve pixel-exact pairing before adversarial training.
  • 02. <1% Minority Fungal Detection (+15% Accuracy): Rebuilt the patch-sampling geometry and applied synthetic data augmentation on NVIDIA GPUs to prevent background collapse.
  • 03. Contrast-Preserving Preprocessing: Wrote custom OpenCV & NumPy normalization pipelines for dark, low-resolution clinical scans.
