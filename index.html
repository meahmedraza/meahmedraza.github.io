RISETech (NUST H-12) · Supervisor: Dr. M. Usman Akram

Fixing spatial label noise and <1% class imbalance

Thin gigapixel biopsy tissue warps during chemical preparation. Intensity-based registration failed, so misaligned patches reached Pix2Pix and DCLGAN and produced blurry, hallucinated cell borders.

  • 160–180 control-point registration in QuPath and Fiji gave pixel-exact pairing before adversarial training.
  • +15% accuracy on a <1% fungal class by rebuilding patch sampling and adding synthetic augmentation on NVIDIA GPUs.
  • Contrast-preserving preprocessing in OpenCV and NumPy for dark, low-resolution clinical scans.
